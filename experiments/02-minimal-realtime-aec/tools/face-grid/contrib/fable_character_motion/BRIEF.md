# Character-animation principles worker

Research how Pixar/Disney character-animation principles and video-game
character animation can be reduced to tiny deterministic procedural motion:
appeal, staging, anticipation, squash/stretch, ease-in/out, overshoot,
follow-through, secondary action, eye lead/head follow, blink phrasing,
listening vs speaking poses, and personality-specific idle loops.

Turn the research into integer-only clock/keyframe motion functions and several
original face studies. Do not reproduce proprietary characters or code. Create
standalone portable C, tests, a motion manifest, and a source/licensing report
in this directory only. Input/output and performance constraints are in
`../README.md`.
