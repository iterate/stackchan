# Wildcard visual-language worker

Find surprising, original, cheap face/presence ideas beyond the obvious:
demoscene, Tamagotchi/virtual pets, rhythm games, fighting-game portraits,
terminal art, oscilloscope/vector displays, CRT masks, LED installations,
comic panels, puppetry, abstract assistants, and accessibility-friendly high
contrast displays. Prioritize genuine algorithmic variety over palette swaps.

Create standalone portable C prototypes, tests, a renderer manifest, and a
source/licensing research report in this directory only. Input/output and
performance constraints are in `../README.md`.
