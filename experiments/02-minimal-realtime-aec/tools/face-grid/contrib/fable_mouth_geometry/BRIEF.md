# Mouth and viseme-geometry worker

Research and prototype visibly different cheap mouth renderers: Preston
Blair-like sprite sets, polygon/JALI-like jaw-lip controls, bezier ribbons,
segmented LED/VU mouths, teeth/tongue shapes, origami masks, and coarticulated
transitions driven only by the semantic keyframe and clock. Include at least
three sprite systems and three polygon systems.

Create standalone portable C, tests, a renderer manifest, and a concise
source/licensing research report in this directory only. Input/output and
performance constraints are in `../README.md`.
