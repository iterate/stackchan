# Eye-study redux review artifacts

Generated by `../../run_face_eye_study_redux_native.sh`; PNG and source PPM are
kept together.

- `eye-study-expressions-8x11`: rows are legacy IDs 15–22; columns are all 11
  stage emotions.
- `eye-study-speech-attention-8x12`: rest, listen, think, STARTING
  anticipation, four ACTIVE energy levels, ENDING settle, low attention, and
  extreme gaze checks.
- `eye-study-blink-chronology-8x24`: 20 ms samples around each profile's
  authored blink minimum.
- `eye-study-saccade-chronology-8x22`: 16 ms samples around a high-motion,
  blink-free autonomous saccade for each profile.

Manual review at original size passed after two concrete corrections:

1. joy, sleepy, and embarrassed apertures were opened enough to remain eyes
   rather than collapsing into accidental tiny dashes;
2. the saccade selector was made blink-free and speech acting was strengthened
   across aperture, lid, width, brow, pupil, and micro-gaze.

The final sheets show stable sockets, clipped pupils/highlights, full-width
authored closed lids, no face translation or accidental crop, all 11 emotions
by geometry, smooth blink closure/recovery, smooth bounded saccades, and
legible anticipation/active/settle speech poses.
